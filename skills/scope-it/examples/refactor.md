# Refactor Example

## Request

Scope a refactor to reduce duplication.

## ScopeIt

✓ Locate the duplicated behavior and its callers.

✓ Define the smallest behavior-preserving boundary.

✓ Identify focused regression tests.

✗ Do not broaden the scope into unrelated cleanup.

✗ Do not introduce speculative abstractions.

✗ Do not perform the refactor.
